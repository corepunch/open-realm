Play fullscreen on iPhone or iPad

Safari cannot make this game's canvas fullscreen in a normal tab. Add OpenRealm to your Home Screen to open it without Safari's browser bars:

  1. Tap Share in Safari (the square with an upward arrow, or More → Share beside the address bar).
  2. Scroll to Add to Home Screen; if offered, leave Open as Web App enabled, then tap Add.
  3. Return to the Home Screen and launch OpenRealm from its icon.

On iPad, Share is generally in Safari's top toolbar. Imported game files may need to be imported again in the Home Screen app because its storage can be separate.
